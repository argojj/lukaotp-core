# Privacy Summary

LukaOTP Core is a local Rust library. It performs TOTP, parsing, encryption,
and portable-backup operations within the calling application's process. The
library has no account system, telemetry, advertising, analytics, network
client, cloud recovery, or multi-device synchronization implementation.

Callers are responsible for their own user interface, storage location,
permissions, retention, transmission, and privacy notices. Do not email OTP
secrets, passwords, backup data, or credentials; privacy questions may be sent
to **devopjj@gmail.com**.
